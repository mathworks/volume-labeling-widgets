Slice Plane Viewer Widget

SlicePlaneViewer is intended to provide orientation information when navigating through a 3D image volume. It shows a plane of image data in each of 3 dimensions at a specified slice in each dimension.

Create the widget

f = uifigure;
g = uigridlayout(f,[1 1]);
w = wt.SlicePlaneViewer(g);

Choose a slice

w.Slice = [150 175 10];
